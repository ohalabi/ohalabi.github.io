Biography

About

Academic and researcher with over 20 years of experience in Virtual Reality (VR), Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), Haptics, and Multimodal Interfaces. Currently an Associate Professor at Qatar University, with over 140 peer-reviewed publications, edited volumes, and a patent spanning extended reality (XR), telexistence systems, and human-centered interactive applications. Recognized among the Top 2% Scientists Worldwide for 2025 by Stanford University and Elsevier.
